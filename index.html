Moves Management for Leadership and Annual Giving

Standard Operating Procedures

Created: September 30, 2022

Portfolio Assignments

Each member of the Development team whose job description includes frontline fundraising responsibility will manage a portfolio of private contributors and prospects. Additional members may have a select group of prospects that merit personalization or specialized attention in their fundraising areas.

Rules, Procedures & Guidelines

  1. Only OppNet Fundraising staff will be assigned as account owners in Salesforce for individuals or entities being managed for fundraising purposes. Other OppNet staff and leadership, including members of the Board of Directors, may play a vital role in the identification, cultivation, solicitation, and stewardship of fundraising prospects, but will not be assigned as owners of these accounts.
  2. Gift officers should review their portfolios at least every three months with the staff managing Development Operations. During these review sessions, the goals should be:

    a. To ensure that current portfolio assignments are appropriate and that reassignments to new owners are made if needed

    b. To ensure that all opportunity stages for prospects are accurate

    c. To adjust the portfolio size by either adding new or unassigned accounts

  3. The gift officer is responsible for updating OR asking Development Associate (cc'ing Senior Development Manager and Senior Director of Development) to update Contact Information as needed on the Contact page. This includes but is not limited to: Name, Title, Phone, Email, Address, and Affiliations.

Opportunities

Opportunities in Salesforce represent prospective and firm asks for charitable contributions in the fundraising pipeline. They are created when a gift officer has a firm lead on a prospect and anticipates making an ask within the FY. For more information on creating an Opportunity, please reference How To - Gift Processing.docx in C:\Users\user\Dropbox (OppNet)\OppNet Development\Documentation, Policies, and Procedures\How To Documentation - Salesforce.

Updating Opportunities

Once an Opportunity has been created, the Gift Officer should update fields above with an (*) for their respective Opportunities in real time OR email this information to the Development Associate asking Development Associate (cc'ing Senior Development Manager and Senior Director of Development) for updating. Updates for all other field updates should go through the Development Associate.

Tasks and Activities (Log a call, Email, etc)

Tasks and activities are an essential component of OppNet's moves management system, ensuring there is a living, dynamic plan to cultivate, solicit, and steward donors and prospective contributors. Tasks are also a means by which the department can accurately measure fundraising progress outside of dollars raised.

  1. Tasks or Activities should be logged after any pertinent information about a prospect or a gift is gleaned, whether through a direct conversation with the prospect or through other means. Examples include:

    a. After a meeting

    b. After a phone or zoom call

    c. Email conversation

    d. Another staff member sends you information

  2. Anytime a task that triggers another action is completed or cancelled, the gift officer is responsible for entering the new task or sending the information to Development Associate (cc'ing Senior Development Manager). If the new task or activity is not logged in real time, the portfolio manager should send the Development Associate their tasks at least once per week.
  3. Tasks can be created either within or outside of Opportunities. When a task is intrinsically related to the cultivation, solicitation, or stewardship of a specific gift, it should be entered from within the Opportunity. When it is unrelated to a specific Opportunity, it should be created on the Contact page.
  4. Tasks should be entered on the Contact or Opportunity through which the fundraising activity is focused. For example, if a gift officer sets up a task to speak with a board member (Jane Doe) to develop a solicitation strategy for a donor (John Smith), the task should be entered in the record of Mr. Smith and not Ms. Doe.

How To – Acknowledgements

For an overview of Apsona Document merge click here

Set up templates in Apsona

  1. Ensure your Word template has the correct fields formatted as merge fields. You will map these later, so the names of the fields do not have to match your source document.
  2. In Apsona, go to “Documents”
  3. Click “Upload”
  4. Select and name your doc, and save your template to the “Acknowledgements” Folder
  5. If you make any changes to your template in Word, replace the template in Apsona

Run an Existing Acknowledgement Process

  1. In Apsona, go to Reporting Tools >> Multi-step reports
  2. Click go to the “Acknowledgements” folder
  3. Click on “Acknowledgment Report” >> Run
  4. The report pulls all paid payments with a status of “Acknowledgement sent for installment = no”
  5. Look at all the campaigns in the report to ensure you have an acknowledgement template to accommodate each gift.
  6. Go back to the “Acknowledgements” folder
  7. For each set of letters that you need to run, click on the corresponding report. E.g. if you are pulling “General Operating” letters, click on the “General Operating” report
  8. After you run the report, click on “Merge/Mail”
  9. Click “Run an existing merge action”
  10. Select the merge action that corresponds to the report. e.g if you are pulling “General Operating” letters, click on the “General Operating” merge action
  11. Click “save and run,” and when the merge completes, download your results.

Create a New Acknowledgement Process

  1. Create a new report with the correct parameters. Most likely, you will be using the “Campaign” as your parameter, so clone an existing Acknowledgement Report, and in payment step’s filter terms, deselect the existing campaign(s) in the “Opportunity Campaign” filters and select the campaign(s) that corresponds to the acknowledgement you are creating.
  2. Click “Save and Run”
  3. CHECK your report to make sure it’s accurate!
  4. Click “Merge/mail”
  5. Select the bubble for “Create a new merge action”
  6. Select the type of doc (for acks, will be word docs)
  7. Click “next”
  8. Select your template from Salesforce Documents
  9. Select “Single file with page breaks”
  10. Click “next”
  11. In “match fields,” map your merge fields in the template to your report fields in Apsona.
  12. Under “set the field,” set the “acknowledgment sent for installment” field as “pending.” This is so you’ll know that after you’ve run the merge process, the letter is being worked on
  13. Click “Save and Run”
  14. Download results

Editing/updating letters:

  • ALWAYS proof your letters, even if your merge fields seem perfect!
  • If a gift comes through a 3rd party processor (Benevity, Amazon Smile, Cybergrants, etc.) OR a Donor Advised Fund (Fidelity Charitable, Goldman Sachs Gives, etc.) remove the tax language at the bottom of the letter.
  • If a portfolio/board member donates through one of these channels, it is often best to craft a warm thank you note for the portfolio manager to have the PM customize and email – donors appreciate this much more than a formal letter*!
    *except sometimes around tax time donors forget through which mechanism they donated and will ask for a formal letter, which we will create but leave off tax language. It’s all a balance
  • If a donor has a strong relationship with OppNet, feel free to customize the language to fit the relationship.
  • In-kind gifts should reference the item(s) donated and use the following tax language:
    The Opportunity Network is a 501(c)3 organization. Our tax ID number is 43-1984494. No goods or services were provided in exchange for your in-kind contribution. Please consult your tax advisor regarding the deductibility of this contribution.
  • Once you are done editing the letters, send to Senior Development Manager for review.
  • For Individual and Corporate acks: once you receive edits and they are incorporated, save the letters as PDFs in the appropriate folder (will follow the below naming schemes; adjust FY):
    • Individual and Corporate: C:\Users\user\Dropbox (OppNet)\OppNet Development\Funders\Individual\Acknowledgment Letters\FY22\Sent
    • Gala: C:\Users\user\Dropbox (OppNet)\OppNet Special Events\Annual Event\Dinner 2022\Acknowledgements
  • Send the acknowledgement via the giving@oppnet email (special events with a cover note such as the below):
    Dear NAME, I hope this finds you healthy and well. I wanted to be in touch today to thank you for GIC's donation to OppNet and send the attached acknowledgement letter for your support. Your [renewed] investment in our work and our students is so appreciated. While we continue working remotely here at OppNet, we are sending our acknowledgement letters via email. If you would prefer to receive a hard copy, please let me know and we would be happy to send one to you in the mail. Thank you again for your support of our students! Best, Signature

Institutional Letters:

  • Letters for funders managed by the Institutional Team are often quite nuanced. Essentially, O & E gets the template started, and then the Institutional Team edits and sends. Please reference what the grant is funding per the grant agreement.
  • Once you’ve edited the letter and it’s been reviewed, send to point person on institutional team (as of 3/11/21 it is Jonelle Carrera). They will save them in the correct folder

OppNet - How To - Merge a Contact

Contact Deduplication Standard Operating Procedures

  1. In App Launcher, search for “contact merge”
  2. Click “search contacts”
  3. Enter your contact’s name
  4. Check up to 3 record to be merged
  5. Click “Next”
  6. Decide which record you want to be the Master Record - this should be the record with the richest and most recent data
  7. Go through the list of fields that have blue and empty circles next to them and select the data you want to retain
    *In this example, Jonelle will replace Katherine as the Contact Owner*
    *If the data from the source record is selected, it will come over and replace the data from the master record
  8. Click merge
  9. You will receive a message that merging is an irreversible process*; click merge
    *The source contact will go into the recycle bin, and you can reinstate them if you need to
  10. You’ll be directed to your newly-merged record!
